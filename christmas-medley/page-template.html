Order, keys and rehearsal notes
  1. Hark! The Herald Angels Sing — E-flat major; includes the Pentatonix version’s riff and bridge.
  2. Mary, Did You Know — C minor; verse 1 and “The blind will see” bridge.
  3. Feliz Navidad — A-flat major; Spanish refrain and English wishes.
  4. The Little Drummer Boy — B-flat major; women sing the verse while men sing the bum background.
  5. Jingle Bell Rock — B-flat major; swing 2:1, including in playback.
  6. This Christmas — E-flat major; verse 1, refrain and brief coda.
  7. All I Want for Christmas Is You — E-flat major; opening taste, fast verse, refrains and bridge.

Written through, once only. Melody in unison or octaves alternates with marked harmony. Short Oo bridges supply new keys. Soprano never exceeds E-flat5. Men sound as written; optional fifths are marked near the end of Little Drummer Boy.
