# Silent Night — source and adaptation notes

Source: the six user-supplied, synchronized 5:11.9 medley rehearsal MP3s and lyrics sheet. Only the ending is used: approximately 4:03.6–5:11.1. The solo introduction precedes the ensemble entrance at approximately 4:17.6. No source recordings are redistributed.

The soprano contour was recovered by comparing the soprano common to the alto, tenor, and bass mixes, with the prominent soprano mix as a cross-check. Alto follows the emphasized alto track. Baritone primarily follows the emphasized tenor, folded into D3–E-flat4 when necessary. Bass was used to inform the harmonic direction. Audio pitch analysis is an aid, not proof of a note-perfect transcription.

This is an editorial SAB adaptation: ornaments and the fastest closing runs are normalized to a readable eighth-note grid; the introduction is regularized to four measures; the piano is a new conventional block-chord accompaniment. The closing arrival on A-flat is retained, and an extra held A-flat chord finishes the medley cutoff. The lyric sheet supplies the verse text; introductory wording and melisma underlay are adapted for this score. It should not be described as an exact transcription of the commercial Temptations recording.

Deliverables: LilyPond editing source, engraved PDF, full clarinet/piano MP3, three isolated clarinet-with-piano MP3s, lyric sheet, and inline score pages. Build with `python build_rehearsal_assets.py silent-night`.
