Soprano, alto & baritone with block piano chords. Adapted from the Silent Night ending of the supplied rehearsal medley.

Solo introduction, harmonized verse, and gospel closing phrases. The soprano follows the source melody with simplified ornaments; the baritone primarily adapts the tenor part. The piano chords and final held chord are newly arranged.

E-flat major, arriving in A-flat major at the close · 67 BPM · approximately 72 seconds. Alto and baritone enter with the verse and use the words under soprano. This is an adapted rehearsal arrangement, rather than an exact transcription of the Temptations recording.
