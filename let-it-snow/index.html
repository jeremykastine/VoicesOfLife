Soprano, alto & baritone with block-chord piano
Words by Sammy Cahn · Music by Jule Styne

The soprano carries the melody, with octave adjustments to low phrases. Alto and baritone join in harmony at selected refrains and responses. The full form includes the scat break and final B-flat modulation. Piano uses simple block chords.
