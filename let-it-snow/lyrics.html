Let It Snow! Let It Snow! Let It Snow!

Words: Sammy Cahn. Music: Jule Styne.

All parts use the same words. Follow the score for entrances and scat rhythms.

A - Verse 1
Oh, the weather outside is frightful,
but the fire is so delightful.
And since we've no place to go,
let it snow, let it snow, let it snow!
B - Verse 2
It doesn't show signs of stopping,
and I've brought some corn for popping.
The lights are turned way down low;
let it snow, let it snow, let it snow!
C - Bridge
When we finally kiss goodnight,
how I'll hate going out in the storm!
But if you'll really hold me tight,
then all the way home I'll be warm.
D - Verse 3
The fire is slowly dying,
and, my dear, we're still goodbying.
But as long as you love me so,
let it snow, let it snow! Let it snow!
E - Scat break (follow the notated syllables)
Do / ba / da / de syllables, then a piano interlude.
F - Reprise
Oh, the weather outside is frightful,
but the fire is so delightful.
And since we've no place to go,
let it snow, let it snow, let it snow!
G - Final verses
The weather outside is frightful,

And since we've no place to go,
let it snow, let it snow, let it snow!
It doesn't show signs of stopping,

let it snow!
H - Final bridge
When we finally kiss goodnight,
how I'll hate going out in the storm!
But if you finally hold me tight,
then all the way home I'll be warm.
I - Final refrain (B-flat)
And, my dear, we're still goodbying.
But as long as you love me so,
let it snow, let it snow!
J - Coda
Let it snow! (Sustain; then piano ending.)
